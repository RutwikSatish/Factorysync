Six Lot Sizing Methods

Every standard algorithm.
Computed simultaneously.

Method 01
Lot-for-Lot
Order = Net Requirements
Orders exactly what's needed each period. Zero excess inventory, maximum ordering frequency. Best when setup cost is very low.
Jacobs & Berry 2011
Method 02
Economic Order Quantity
Q* = √(2DS/H)
Classic square-root formula. Balances ordering and holding costs assuming steady demand. Overstocks when demand is lumpy.
Jacobs & Berry p. 94
Method 03
Period Order Quantity
P = round(EOQ / avg demand)
Orders every P periods, covering the next P periods of demand. Adapts EOQ to discrete time buckets with regular rhythm.
Jacobs & Berry p. 95
Method 04
Part Period Balancing
EPP = S/H · balance cumulative holding ≈ S
Accumulates demand until cumulative holding cost approaches the ordering cost. Practical heuristic available in most ERP systems.
Jacobs & Berry p. 96
Method 05
Silver-Meal Heuristic
min C(T) = (S + Σ holding) / T
Minimizes average cost per period. Stop extending the order window when marginal cost increases. Research shows <8% penalty vs. optimal.
Silver & Meal (1973) Mgmt. Sci.
Method 06
Wagner-Whitin
f[t] = min { S + holding(t→j) + f[j+1] }
Dynamic programming benchmark. Globally optimal O(n²). Rarely implemented in ERP systems. Used here as the lower bound.
Wagner & Whitin (1958) Mgmt. Sci.
